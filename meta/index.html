Privacy policy: ads-automation-test (Meta app)

Last updated: 22 October 2026

ads-automation-test is a personal developer tool built and run by Akmal Dzakwan Faiz. Its Meta app manages ad campaigns through the Meta Marketing API on ad accounts and Facebook Pages owned by the developer's own business portfolio. It has no other users, no Facebook Login for the public and no web interface, and it never accesses other people's accounts or Pages.

Data the tool accesses

  • The developer's own ad account details: name, id, currency and status.
  • Campaigns, ad sets, ads and ad creatives in that ad account, with their budgets, bids, targeting settings and statuses.
  • The developer's own Facebook Page id and name, used as the identity of the ads.
  • Campaign reports (insights): spend, impressions, clicks and conversions.

The tool does not request or store any personal data about people who see or click the ads.

How the data is used

Only to create, change, pause and report on the developer's own campaigns. Reports may be read by an AI assistant (Claude by Anthropic) that the developer uses to operate the tool on his own machine.

Where the data is kept

On the developer's own computer. The access token is kept in a local file that only the developer's user can read. A local log records each change the tool makes (time, object id, old and new values). Data is kept only as long as the developer needs it for managing and testing his campaigns.

Sharing

No data is sold. No data is shared with third parties for their own use.

Data deletion

To delete data this app holds about you, open an issue at github.com/AzafaDev/ads-automation-test/issues with the title "Data deletion request". The developer deletes the local data within 30 days and confirms in the issue. Since the app only works with the developer's own accounts, it normally holds no data about anyone else. You can also remove the app's access in your Facebook settings under Business integrations, which ends all access.

Contact

Through the issues of this site's repository: github.com/AzafaDev/ads-automation-test/issues.
